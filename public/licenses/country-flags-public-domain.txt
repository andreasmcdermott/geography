Country flag artwork

Source: country-flags by Hampus Joakim Borgos
https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags

The source project describes these accurate SVG flag renders as public-domain
material, primarily sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Country flags may still be
subject to non-copyright restrictions under local law.

The SVGs are presented at their native proportions so that dimensions that are
part of a flag's official design remain visible in the quiz.
